This project builds a peer-comparable ESG rating framework from scratch: it ingests company-disclosed data (BRSR filings, sustainability reports, annual reports), converts raw performance values into normalized 0-100 metric scores, rolls those up into Environmental / Social / Governance / Transparency dimension scores, and produces a single defensible Overall ESG Score and letter rating (AAA–CCC) for every company.The workbook is intentionally built as a transparent, auditable scoring engine rather than a black-box rating: every number on the dashboard can be traced back through one visible formula to one disclosed data point.Business ProblemBuyers, investors, and internal sustainability teams evaluating Indian textile suppliers face three recurring problems:No common yardstick — each company reports ESG data in a different format (BRSR, GRI, TCFD, sustainability report, integrated annual report), making direct comparison difficult.Inconsistent disclosure completeness — some companies disclose granular Scope 1/2 emissions and water intensity, others disclose almost nothing, and a naive scoring model would either penalize non-disclosure unfairly or silently ignore it.Opaque third-party ESG scores — commercial ESG ratings are often a black box, making it hard for an analyst to explain why one company scored higher than another.Project ObjectiveBuild a from-scratch, fully transparent ESG scoring model and dashboard that:Converts heterogeneous, company-disclosed ESG data into one standardized 0-100 score per company.Makes every scoring rule (weights, normalization method, tier lookups) explicit and auditable.Flags data confidence separately from the score itself, so a high score backed by complete disclosure is distinguishable from a high score based on partial data.Presents results through an executive-friendly dashboard for both single-company review and portfolio-wide benchmarking.Dashboard Features1.
